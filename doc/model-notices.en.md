# Comic translation models and licenses

Open **Settings → AI Translation → Model management** to download the files you need. All languages share one detector to locate text. Choose a recognizer and its matching dictionary for the comic’s original language. **Dialogue only** can use built-in rules or the optional RT-DETR detector. Detection, recognition, default text removal and translated text layout run on your device. Text removal and layout require no additional model. You configure the translation service separately. Its provider sets its terms, pricing and handling of the text you submit. [User guide](guide.en.md) · [简体中文](model-notices.zh.md)

## Downloads and publishers

| Purpose | Files and publishers |
| --- | --- |
| Text location (default detector) | PaddlePaddle publishes the PP-OCRv5 mobile weights as `inference.onnx`. The model card identifies Apache-2.0. The app downloads revision `df0bd9dee2bc627e80a2a1798ccab35a332e22d6` and verifies the SHA-256 digest. [Official files](https://huggingface.co/PaddlePaddle/PP-OCRv5_mobile_det_onnx/tree/df0bd9dee2bc627e80a2a1798ccab35a332e22d6) · [License declaration](https://huggingface.co/PaddlePaddle/PP-OCRv5_mobile_det_onnx/blob/df0bd9dee2bc627e80a2a1798ccab35a332e22d6/README.md) |
| Text location (optional detector) | RapidAI/RapidOCR publishes PP-OCRv6 small as `PP-OCRv6_det_small.onnx` on ModelScope. [RapidAI files](https://modelscope.cn/models/RapidAI/RapidOCR/files) |
| English and Korean recognition | SWHL/RapidOCR publishes the English PP-OCRv3 weights `en_PP-OCRv3_rec_infer.onnx` and the Korean mobile v2.0 weights `korean_mobile_v2.0_rec_infer.onnx`. Their respective dictionaries, `en_dict.txt` and `korean_dict.txt`, come from PaddleOCR v2.7.0. [Weights](https://huggingface.co/SWHL/RapidOCR/tree/main) · [English dictionary](https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR/blob/v2.7.0/ppocr/utils/en_dict.txt) · [Korean dictionary](https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR/blob/v2.7.0/ppocr/utils/dict/korean_dict.txt) |
| Simplified and Traditional Chinese recognition | RapidAI/RapidOCR publishes the PP-OCRv5 mobile weights `ch_PP-OCRv5_rec_mobile.onnx` and dictionary `ppocrv5_dict.txt` on ModelScope. The app tries that source first. The fallback, webnn/PP-OCRv5-ONNX, publishes `ch_PP-OCRv5_rec.onnx` and `ch_PP-OCRv5_dict.txt`. Both repositories identify Apache-2.0. The same recognizer reads both scripts. [RapidAI files](https://modelscope.cn/models/RapidAI/RapidOCR/files) · [webnn model card and files](https://huggingface.co/webnn/PP-OCRv5-ONNX/tree/main) |
| Japanese manga recognition | mayocream publishes `encoder_model.onnx`, `decoder_model.onnx` and the `vocab.txt` vocabulary. The source model is kha-white/manga-ocr-base. The source and ONNX model cards both identify Apache-2.0. [Source model](https://huggingface.co/kha-white/manga-ocr-base) · [ONNX files and vocabulary](https://huggingface.co/mayocream/manga-ocr-onnx/tree/main) |
| Latin, Cyrillic and Thai recognition presets | RapidAI/RapidOCR publishes three PP-OCRv5 mobile recognizers with matching dictionaries on ModelScope. The files are `<code>_PP-OCRv5_rec_mobile.onnx` and `ppocrv5_<code>_dict.txt`, where the code is `latin`, `cyrillic` or `th`. **Model management** lets you choose a preset or enter custom file URLs. [RapidAI files](https://modelscope.cn/models/RapidAI/RapidOCR/files) |
| Optional dialogue detection | `detector-v4-s_int8.onnx` by ogkalu, the author of Comic Translate, is pinned to `16e8a622f91fabc6b5b65c96d32d1183f8843546`. The model card at that revision identifies Apache-2.0 and its file list includes this INT8 export. Turning the enhancement off keeps the file. **All text** skips this model. [Pinned model card](https://huggingface.co/ogkalu/comic-text-and-bubble-detector/blob/16e8a622f91fabc6b5b65c96d32d1183f8843546/README.md) · [Pinned weights](https://huggingface.co/ogkalu/comic-text-and-bubble-detector/blob/16e8a622f91fabc6b5b65c96d32d1183f8843546/detector-v4-s_int8.onnx) |

Hugging Face downloads default to its official site. Selecting `hf-mirror.com` changes requests that use this endpoint. ModelScope and dictionary CDN downloads follow their per-file configuration. When you supply a custom detector URL or a recognition model and dictionary URL, check the terms supplied by each publisher. Most OCR weights and vocabularies follow their publisher's `main` or `master` branch and a later download may retrieve a newer release. [Download configuration](https://github.com/Kyosee/VeneraX/blob/master/lib/foundation/image_translation/translation_models.dart)

Earlier versions offered the PP-OCRv4 text detector and Chinese / Latin recognizer published by SWHL/RapidOCR. Both third-party ONNX files have been removed from the model library. If the files are still on your device, open **My models → Older model files** to see their size or remove them.

## Licensing, training data and redistribution

- **Models and dictionaries.** RapidOCR's Models statement attributes the upstream PaddleOCR weights to Baidu and/or the respective PaddleOCR rights holders. It says the applicable ONNX conversions it publishes retain Apache-2.0. The PaddleOCR v2.7.0 repository includes Apache-2.0. For the other exports, the table links the publishers' own model cards. Rights in training images, comics, fonts and corpora remain with their respective rights holders. [RapidOCR model statement](https://github.com/RapidAI/RapidOCR#models) · [PaddleOCR v2.7.0 license](https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR/blob/v2.7.0/LICENSE)
- **Japanese model training data.** The Manga OCR author credits Manga109-s and CC-100. The Manga109-s terms permit commercial use of experimental results, require acknowledgement of Manga109-s when publishing research results including pretrained models, and prohibit redistribution of the dataset itself. The CC-100 publisher credits Common Crawl as its data source and asks users to cite the listed research papers. VeneraX downloads the recognition model and vocabulary. [Author acknowledgements](https://github.com/kha-white/manga-ocr#acknowledgments) · [Manga109-s terms](https://huggingface.co/datasets/hal-utokyo/Manga109-s) · [CC-100 distribution](https://data.statmt.org/cc-100/)
