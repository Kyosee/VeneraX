# Comic translation models and licenses

VeneraX downloads the model weights listed below on demand after installation. Choose files in **Settings → AI Translation → Translation models** to download them from the listed publisher or your selected mirror. They run on your device. Default text removal works directly on the image. **Dialogue only** can use built-in rules or the optional RT-DETR detector. You configure the translation service separately. Its provider sets its terms, pricing and handling of the text you submit. [User guide](guide.en.md) · [简体中文](model-notices.zh.md)

## Downloads and publishers

| Purpose | Files and publishers |
| --- | --- |
| Default text detection | PaddleOCR's PP-OCRv4 mobile detector, distributed by SWHL/RapidOCR as `ch_PP-OCRv4_det_infer.onnx`. [Published files](https://huggingface.co/SWHL/RapidOCR/tree/main/PP-OCRv4) |
| Simplified Chinese and Latin, English, and Korean recognition | SWHL/RapidOCR provides `ch_PP-OCRv4_rec_infer.onnx`, `en_PP-OCRv3_rec_infer.onnx` and `korean_mobile_v2.0_rec_infer.onnx`, respectively. The Korean v2.0 file is in the publisher's `PP-OCRv1` directory. The corresponding dictionaries are from PaddleOCR v2.7.0, served via jsDelivr or GitHub as configured per file. [ONNX files](https://huggingface.co/SWHL/RapidOCR/tree/main) · [Chinese dictionary](https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR/blob/v2.7.0/ppocr/utils/ppocr_keys_v1.txt) · [English](https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR/blob/v2.7.0/ppocr/utils/en_dict.txt) · [Korean](https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR/blob/v2.7.0/ppocr/utils/dict/korean_dict.txt) |
| Simplified and Traditional Chinese recognition | The PP-OCRv5 mobile recognizer and dictionary come first from RapidAI/RapidOCR on ModelScope. The alternative webnn/PP-OCRv5-ONNX repository publishes `ch_PP-OCRv5_rec.onnx` and `ch_PP-OCRv5_dict.txt`. Both repositories identify Apache-2.0. [RapidAI files](https://modelscope.cn/models/RapidAI/RapidOCR/files) · [webnn model card and files](https://huggingface.co/webnn/PP-OCRv5-ONNX/tree/main) |
| Japanese manga recognition | mayocream publishes `encoder_model.onnx`, `decoder_model.onnx` and the `vocab.txt` vocabulary. The source model is kha-white/manga-ocr-base. The source and ONNX model cards both identify Apache-2.0. [Source model](https://huggingface.co/kha-white/manga-ocr-base) · [ONNX files and vocabulary](https://huggingface.co/mayocream/manga-ocr-onnx/tree/main) |
| Optional recognition and detection presets | RapidAI/RapidOCR on ModelScope provides three PP-OCRv5 mobile recognition presets for Latin (`latin`), Cyrillic (`cyrillic`) and Thai (`th`), each with its matching `ppocrv5_<code>_dict.txt`. The two detector presets are `ch_PP-OCRv5_det_mobile.onnx` and `PP-OCRv6_det_small.onnx`. [RapidAI files](https://modelscope.cn/models/RapidAI/RapidOCR/files) · [Preset configuration](https://github.com/Kyosee/VeneraX/blob/master/lib/pages/settings/translation_languages_settings.dart) |
| Optional dialogue detection | `detector-v4-s_int8.onnx` by ogkalu, the author of Comic Translate, is pinned to `16e8a622f91fabc6b5b65c96d32d1183f8843546`. The model card at that revision identifies Apache-2.0 and its file list includes this INT8 export. Turning the enhancement off keeps the file. **All text** skips this model. [Pinned model card](https://huggingface.co/ogkalu/comic-text-and-bubble-detector/blob/16e8a622f91fabc6b5b65c96d32d1183f8843546/README.md) · [Pinned weights](https://huggingface.co/ogkalu/comic-text-and-bubble-detector/blob/16e8a622f91fabc6b5b65c96d32d1183f8843546/detector-v4-s_int8.onnx) |

Hugging Face downloads default to its official site. Selecting `hf-mirror.com` changes requests that use this endpoint. ModelScope and dictionary CDN downloads follow their per-file configuration. When you supply a custom detector URL or a recognition model and dictionary URL, check the terms supplied by each publisher. Most OCR weights and vocabularies follow their publisher's `main` or `master` branch and a later download may retrieve a newer release. [Download configuration](https://github.com/Kyosee/VeneraX/blob/master/lib/foundation/image_translation/translation_models.dart)

## Licensing, training data and redistribution

- **Models and dictionaries.** RapidOCR's Models statement attributes the upstream PaddleOCR weights to Baidu and/or the respective PaddleOCR rights holders. It says the applicable ONNX conversions it publishes retain Apache-2.0. The PaddleOCR v2.7.0 repository includes Apache-2.0. For the other exports, the table links the publishers' own model cards. Rights in training images, comics, fonts and corpora remain with their respective rights holders. [RapidOCR model statement](https://github.com/RapidAI/RapidOCR#models) · [PaddleOCR v2.7.0 license](https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR/blob/v2.7.0/LICENSE)
- **Japanese model training data.** The Manga OCR author credits Manga109-s and CC-100. The Manga109-s terms permit commercial use of experimental results, require acknowledgement of Manga109-s when publishing research results including pretrained models, and prohibit redistribution of the dataset itself. The CC-100 publisher credits Common Crawl as its data source and asks users to cite the listed research papers. VeneraX downloads the recognition model and vocabulary. [Author acknowledgements](https://github.com/kha-white/manga-ocr#acknowledgments) · [Manga109-s terms](https://huggingface.co/datasets/hal-utokyo/Manga109-s) · [CC-100 distribution](https://data.statmt.org/cc-100/)
