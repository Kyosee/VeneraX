## What's Changed

### Added
- Added a random comic draw to pick something from your favorites when you cannot decide what to read
- The draw can be limited by favorite folder and reading progress (not started / in progress / finished), and never repeats a comic within the same round
- Migrating comics to an online library can now skip comics whose folder already exists there, and reports how many were skipped

### Fixed
- Fixed already translated images not showing their translation in the reader and being translated all over again
- Fixed local comics occasionally loading a blank image; the file is now retried, and a clear error is shown when it truly cannot be read
