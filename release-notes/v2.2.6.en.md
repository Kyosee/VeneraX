## What's Changed

### Added
- Added a reading statistics page showing today's reading time, the last seven days, total reading time, a seven-day trend, and comics read in the last 30 days
- Reading time is now tracked automatically while reading
- Added an option for WebDAV libraries to detect linked comic folders, including folders some servers report as files

### Changed
- Comic details can now be refreshed manually, including local comics whose metadata, cover, or chapters changed on disk
- WebDAV migration now checks local chapters before upload and shows detailed failure information, including the affected comic and chapter

### Fixed
- Fixed opening a favorite chapter at a specific index incorrectly applying the saved chapter-group offset
- Fixed notification links opening content outside the safe area on some devices
- Fixed reader menu and system UI changes occasionally destabilizing the reading viewport or page loading
- Fixed images and chapters occasionally getting stuck loading indefinitely
- Fixed rapid page turns in continuous reading jumping to incorrect positions or becoming stuck
